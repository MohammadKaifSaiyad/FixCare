# Naming & Branding

## Platform Name

**FixCare**

### Why FixCare Works

- **Fix** — Action, clear what we do
- **Care** — Trust signal, warmth, ongoing relationship
- Easy to pronounce in Gujarati, Hindi, English
- Works pan-India for future scaling
- Two syllables, memorable
- Trademark-friendly (coined compound)

### App Naming Pattern

Following the Uber/Swiggy/Zomato three-app pattern:

| App | Audience | Name |
|---|---|---|
| Customer app | Homeowners | **FixCare** |
| Technician app | Technicians | **FixCare Pro** |
| Merchant flow | Hardware shops | **FixCare Partner** (web/WhatsApp in V1) |

---

## Pre-Launch Verification Checklist

Before locking the name, confirm:

- [ ] Domain available: `fixcare.in`, `fixcare.com`, `fixcare.app`
- [ ] Trademark search at [ipindia.gov.in](https://ipindia.gov.in/)
  - Class 37 (repair services)
  - Class 42 (software)
- [ ] No similar app on Play Store / App Store (India)
- [ ] No negative associations on Google search
- [ ] Social handles available: Instagram, X, YouTube
- [ ] Pronunciation test: 10 non-tech Vadodara locals repeat correctly

### Fallback Names (If FixCare Taken)

In priority order:
1. FixCare.app
2. GetFixCare
3. FixCareIndia
4. SahiHaath (Hindi/Gujarati alternative — strong trust signal)

## Tagline Direction

Working taglines (pick later after user testing):

- "Fix Kar, Bharosa Ke Saath." (Fix it, with trust)
- "Sahi Kaam, Sahi Daam." (Right work, right price)
- "Your Home in Trusted Hands."
- "Verified Pros, Fair Prices, Zero Stress."

---

## Brand Voice

- **Honest** — Never overpromise. Show prices upfront.
- **Warm** — Use everyday language, not corporate-speak
- **Bilingual-friendly** — Communications in simple Hindi/Gujarati/English mix
- **Trust-forward** — Lead with verification, reviews, warranties

---

## Visual Direction (To Decide With Designer)

Likely directions:
- Primary color: trustworthy (deep blue, teal, or warm orange)
- Avoid: red (alarm), neon (cheap feel)
- Logo: hand/tool/checkmark combination
- Typography: rounded, friendly, readable on low-end Android screens

**Don't lock visual identity solo.** Hire a designer for V1 logo + style guide (~₹15-25k).
